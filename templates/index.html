How RazorRecover AI Works

1. 10,000+ Synthetic Merchant Dataset: A Supabase PostgreSQL database initializes automatically with realistic customer histories across UPI, Cards, Net Banking, and Wallets.

2. Dynamic Calculations: All metrics—including Potential Loss, Recovered Revenue, and Recovery Rate—are calculated live from the database via SQL queries.

3. Explainable AI Scoring: Diagnoses failure reasons and customer loyalty to assign high, medium, or low recovery probabilities with human-readable rationale.

4. Stopping Rules & Guardrails: Halts recovery workflows after 3 attempts or when probability is under 25% to protect customer experience.

5. Verified Audit Trail: Every single AI decision, merchant approval, and transaction update is logged with exact timestamps.
